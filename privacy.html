Cookies and Other Data Collection Tools

Cookies are small text files placed by a website and stored by your browser on your device. A cookie can later be read when your browser connects to a web server in the same domain that placed the cookie. The text in a cookie contains a string of numbers and letters that may uniquely identify your device and can contain other information, as well. This allows the web server to recognize your browser over time, each time it connects to that web server.

We do not currently use web beacons, pixels, or similar tracking technologies on our Services, and we do not deploy third-party advertising or social media pixels or tracking tools.

How do we and our partners use cookies and similar technologies?

We and our service providers use cookies and local storage in our Services for essential and functional purposes-such as maintaining your session, enabling authentication and sign-in, and remembering your settings and preferences-including through our hosting, scheduling, and payment providers (e.g., AWS, Acuity, and Stripe). We also use these technologies to recognize users, analyze how our Services perform, and combat fraud. We do not use these technologies for interest-based or cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or marketing technologies. We do not permit third-party tracking technologies on pages where you submit health-related information. We do not share the data collected through these technologies with third parties for advertising purposes.

Cookie controls. Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default. If you prefer, you can go to your browser settings to learn how to delete or reject cookies. If you choose to delete or reject cookies, this could affect certain features or services of the Platform, including sign-in and session functionality. To find out more about cookies, including how to manage and delete cookies, visit ww.allaboutcookies.org.

Do Not Track. Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting intended to signal that you do not wish to be tracked. Because there is no common standard for interpreting DNT signals, the Platform does not respond to them. You can instead use the cookie controls described above to manage certain data collection.
