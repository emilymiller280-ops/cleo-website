Our Story

Cleo's Story

Cleo was born from a personal observation: women are too often left to navigate life's biggest health transitions with far less support than they deserve. Growing up in a large, female-dominated family and watching friends become mothers, Emily saw how fragmented it was to find trusted care beyond the OB-GYN or fertility specialist.

A background in neuroscience, years working alongside women in entertainment, and time living in London, where pregnancy and postpartum care looked very different, all pointed to the same truth: exceptional care already existed. It just wasn't easy to find. Women deserve a more thoughtful, human-centered experience.

We're on a mission to make your life easier.

Finding great care shouldn't be this hard.

Cleo makes finding trusted care simple, personal and effortless, connecting every woman with the right support exactly when she needs it, so she can navigate her health with confidence.

Let's find the care that's right for you.

Tell us where you are in your journey, and we'll match you with a trusted provider.
