Why Cleo

Maternal health is underserved.

1 in 5

Women experience perinatal mood disorders, a rate that has more than doubled since 2010.

75%

Of postpartum depression cases go undiagnosed or untreated, one of the most common, and most overlooked, complications of pregnancy.

40%

Of women undergoing fertility treatment meet the criteria for clinical depression or anxiety, comparable to cancer patients.

12 weeks

The shortest average maternity leave among developed nations, leaving mothers to recover with little structured support.

Zero

Mandated postpartum home visits in the US. We're the only high-income country without them.

Highest

The US has the highest maternal mortality rate of any high-income nation, and it's been rising.

Three pillars of care

Care for the whole woman.

While all aspects of wellbeing are connected, we support you through three interconnected pillars, at every stage.

Physical

Your body carries an enormous amount through fertility, pregnancy, and birth. We coordinate targeted bodywork, massage, acupuncture, and chef-prepared meals tailored to where you are.

Emotional

The emotional weight of this journey is real, and often invisible. Licensed mental health therapists, night care, and ongoing support ensure no part is carried alone.

Connection

From fertility advocates and birth doulas to lactation consultants, our network is built to guide and connect. Because it's easier when you're not doing it alone.
