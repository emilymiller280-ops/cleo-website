Why Cleo

Maternal health is underserved.

1 in 5

Women experience perinatal mood disorders, a rate that has more than doubled since 2010.1

1 in 6

People worldwide are affected by infertility, yet the path to support is rarely clear or coordinated.2

40%

Of women undergoing fertility treatment meet the criteria for clinical depression or anxiety, comparable to cancer patients.3

Only one

High-income country with no national paid parental leave. Federal law guarantees just 12 weeks, unpaid.4

Zero

Guaranteed postpartum home visits in the US. We're the only wealthy nation that doesn't provide them.5

Highest

The US has the highest maternal mortality rate of any high-income nation, and it's been rising.6

  1. Prevalence: Policy Center for Maternal Mental Health, Maternal Mental Health Fact Sheet. Rise since 2010: Health Affairs, 2023 (PMAD diagnoses up ~93%, 2008–2020).
  2. World Health Organization, 2023 (roughly 1 in 6 adults worldwide experience infertility in their lifetime).
  3. Fertility & Sterility (depression in fertility patients); comparison to cancer/HIV patients per Domar et al., Harvard Medical School.
  4. Pew Research Center / OECD; 12 weeks unpaid via the federal FMLA.
  5. The Commonwealth Fund, 2020.
  6. The Commonwealth Fund, 2024.

Our Network

Everything your journey requires.

A comprehensive maternal care system, vetted, in-home or on your terms. Our goal is to make your healthcare feel intuitive, informed, and empowering rather than overwhelming.

Your body carries an enormous amount through fertility, pregnancy, and birth. We coordinate targeted bodywork, massage, acupuncture, and chef-prepared meals tailored to where you are.

The weight of this journey is real, and often invisible. Licensed mental health therapists and ongoing support ensure no part is carried alone.

From birth doulas to lactation consultants, our network is built to guide and connect. Because it's easier when you're not doing it alone.
