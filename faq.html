Human first, always.

Some of life's biggest moments come with questions, uncertainty, and the need for support that feels both trusted and personal. Cleo was created to make that support easier to find. By thoughtfully connecting women with trusted, personalized care, we remove the burden of navigating a fragmented system, whether they're returning to work after maternity leave or exploring a less traditional path to care.

Cleo offers clarity, guidance, and trusted options without prescribing a single way forward. Because support shouldn't be something you have to search for; it should simply be there when you need it.

Beyond expertise, Cleo is deeply committed to making every woman feel seen, understood, and cared for. Every interaction is designed to provide comfort, reassurance, and personalized support throughout every stage of her health journey.

Services & Providers

Every provider on Cleo is individually reviewed or invited. We're either referred to individuals or practices through other clients, providers, and doctors, or practitioners apply to join. That application covers their specialties, degrees and certifications, license number and states licensed, years in practice, and how they work. Each application is reviewed personally, interviews are conducted, and references are verified (where necessary) before anyone is invited into the network.

Every provider then signs a Platform Services Agreement in which they represent that they hold, and will maintain, all licensure, credentials, and training required in every jurisdiction where they practice, and that they'll notify us immediately if that changes. They're also required to carry malpractice and cyber-liability insurance and to provide proof on request.

We offer birth and postpartum doula support, lactation consulting, chef-prepared meal delivery, massage therapy, acupuncture, pelvic floor PT, bodywork, mental health therapy, night care - all coordinated through your care concierge. If there's another service you're curious about, please let us know here.

Most Cleo services are delivered in-home, but some providers (like lactation consultants and mental health therapists) are also available virtually. Your concierge will confirm the format that works best for each service in your plan.

Pricing & Gifting

You pay for the care you book, nothing more. There are no packages, memberships, or subscriptions to sign up for. Each provider sets the rate for their services, and we share clear pricing with you before anything is confirmed. A standing Cleo appointment fee is added to each booking to cover the coordination and support we provide. Book a single session or as much ongoing support as you'd like, and add more care whenever you need it.

Cleo makes a deeply meaningful gift for any mother in your life, whether she's trying to conceive, expecting, or navigating postpartum. You can gift a specific service or an open credit she can put toward the care she needs most. Send a gift here, and we'll take it from there.

Mental health support, pelvic floor physical therapy and lactation consultations may be covered under your insurance plan*. Once booked, we help check your eligibility where applicable and navigate insurance related headache for you. We can assist you in understanding what questions to ask your insurer, what documentation to request from providers, and how to submit for reimbursement where applicable.

Logistics

Your Cleo concierge is your single point of contact for everything. They coordinate scheduling with all practitioners, handle any changes or logistics, check in on how things are going, and adjust your plan as needed.

We understand that plans change, especially around pregnancy. Our extensive network allows us to be flexible and work with unexpected circumstances, so if your baby arrives early or your timeline shifts, we'll adapt your plan accordingly. Just reach out to your concierge and we'll take it from there.
