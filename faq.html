Getting Started

Cleo is a full-spectrum maternal care concierge for women and families navigating fertility, pregnancy, and postpartum. We coordinate in-home care, wellness treatments, meal delivery, and mental health support through a single point of contact. We're for anyone on the maternal journey who wants expert, personalized care without the chaos of vetting and coordinating it themselves.

Book care directly below.

Book Care

We currently serve Los Angeles County, Orange County, and Santa Barbara County. If you're located outside these areas, please contact us and we'll do our best to accommodate your needs or connect you with trusted resources in your area.

Services & Providers

Every practitioner in our network has been personally vetted for their credentials, experience, and commitment to compassionate maternal care. We only partner with providers who share our mission of supporting mothers through every stage of their journey.

We offer birth and postpartum doula support, lactation consulting, chef-prepared meal delivery, massage therapy, acupuncture, pelvic floor PT, bodywork, mental health therapy, night care - all coordinated through your care concierge. If there's another service you're curious about, please let us know here.

Most Cleo services are delivered in-home, but some providers (like lactation consultants and mental health therapists) are also available virtually. Your concierge will confirm the format that works best for each service in your plan.

Packages & Pricing

Packages are pre-designed plans based on the number of weeks you would like to use our services and your choice of Essential, Signature, or Premium tier. All packages include meal delivery, mental health support, and wellness treatments. Higher tiers layer in additional services like doula support, night care, and more.

Individual bookings are also an option for families that would like to choose specific offerings. For both options, booking will be through our bespoke concierge service so that they can handle the scheduling directly with practitioners. All communication will be streamlined through one point of contact.

Cleo makes a deeply meaningful gift for any mother in your life, whether she's trying to conceive, expecting, or navigating postpartum. We offer gift options across both individual bookings and full packages, from a single session to a complete care plan. Book care here. We'll take it from there.

Packages start at $200 per week and are designed around your journey stage and timeline. A sample Essential Package might include weekly meal delivery, a wellness treatment, and mental health support. Signature ($380/wk) and Premium ($550/wk) tiers layer in doula support, night care, acupuncture, and more, all coordinated through your concierge. Every package is customized to your needs.

Mental health support, pelvic floor physical therapy and lactation consultations may be covered under your insurance plan*. Once booked, we help check your eligibility where applicable and navigate insurance related headache for you. We can assist you in understanding what questions to ask your insurer, what documentation to request from providers, and how to submit for reimbursement where applicable.

Logistics

Your Cleo concierge is your single point of contact for everything. They coordinate scheduling with all practitioners, handle any changes or logistics, check in on how things are going, and adjust your plan as needed.

We understand that plans change, especially around pregnancy. Our extensive network allows us to be flexible and work with unexpected circumstances, so if your baby arrives early or your timeline shifts, we'll adapt your plan accordingly. Just reach out to your concierge and we'll take it from there.
